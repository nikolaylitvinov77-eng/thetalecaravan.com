Opossum Brings Fire

Mexico · Episode 5 · Cora myth

Tonight, the caravan stops in Mexico.

Long ago, fire burned only in the sky, on a rock no bird could reach. Opossum climbs all night, through a waterfall, to the old Grandpa who sits by the fire.

About the tale

A myth of the Cora people of the state of Nayarit, Mexico. K. Th. Preuss printed Cora tellings of it in German translation in “Die Nayarit-Expedition”, vol. 1, “Die Religion der Cora-Indianer” (Leipzig: Teubner, 1912): the fire episode at the end of the myth “Der göttliche Sämann” and the separate story “Der Feuerraub”.

A myth of the Cora people of Nayarit, Mexico, retold from K. Th. Preuss, “Die Nayarit-Expedition” (1912).
