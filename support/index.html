Support the Caravan

Help us bring the world’s stories to life.

We are building a free, interactive library of folktales from 195 countries.

Every story is a window into another culture.

With your support, we can bring more stories to children and families around the world.

One world. 195 countries. Endless stories.

How you can help

  • Help us make new tales. Every new episode takes time and money. If you or your organisation would like to support the caravan, write to us and we will tell you how.
  • Help without money. Research and sources, translations, advice from folklorists, museum materials — all of this keeps the tales true to their cultures.

What stays the same

  • Watching the tales is free for everyone.
  • Supporters do not decide what the tales say.
  • There are no ads inside the cartoons. We thank our supporters here, on the site, on the page The People Behind the Stories.
